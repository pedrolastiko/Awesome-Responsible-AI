# Responsible AI glossary

- **Accountability:** Assignment of clear responsibility for AI system outcomes and controls.
- **Explainability:** Ability to provide understandable reasons behind model behavior.
- **Harmful bias:** Systematic and unjustified disparities in AI outcomes across groups.
- **Human oversight:** Human authority to supervise, intervene, or override AI outputs.
- **Model card:** Structured documentation of model intent, performance, limitations, and risks.
- **Risk register:** Living inventory of identified AI risks, owners, controls, and mitigation status.
- **Transparency:** Clear disclosure of AI usage, capabilities, and limitations.
