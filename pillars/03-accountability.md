# Pillar 03 — Accountability

## Why it matters

Accountability clarifies who is responsible for AI decisions, controls, and remediation throughout the lifecycle.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Are roles and ownership defined across legal, risk, product, and engineering teams?
- Is decision evidence maintained (approvals, exceptions, incident logs)?
- Are escalation and remediation paths documented?
- Is board or executive oversight established for high-impact systems?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How accountability is addressed |
|---|---|
| OECD due diligence guidance | Clarifies organizational responsibilities and risk management actions. |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Requires management system accountability and continual improvement. |
| EU AI governance models | Define provider/deployer obligations and oversight responsibilities. |
| Transparency reports (industry) | Public accountability through governance and risk disclosures. |
