# Pillar 02 — Fairness

## Why it matters

Fairness reduces discriminatory outcomes and helps maintain legitimacy, legal defensibility, and social trust.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Have protected groups and impacted communities been identified?
- Are fairness metrics selected and justified for the context?
- Are mitigation actions documented across data, model, and usage layers?
- Is there a process for contesting harmful outcomes?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How fairness is addressed |
|---|---|
| UNESCO/OECD principles | Emphasize non-discrimination and equitable outcomes. |
| ISO/IEC 24027 | Provides guidance on bias in AI systems and AI-aided decisions. |
| NIST AI RMF | Encourages measurement and management of harmful bias. |
| Industry RAI programs | Often include fairness testing playbooks and review gates. |

## Recommended tools or checklists

- Bias impact assessment checklist
- Dataset representativeness review
- Fairness metric selection worksheet
