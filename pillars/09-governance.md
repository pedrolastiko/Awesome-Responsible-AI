# Pillar 09 — Governance

## Why it matters

Governance operationalizes all other pillars through policies, controls, decision rights, and continuous improvement.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Is there a documented AI governance model (centralized, federated, hybrid)?
- Are risk registers, control owners, and review cadences defined?
- Are internal policies established for use, development, procurement, and monitoring?
- Are audits and transparency reports used for continuous improvement?
