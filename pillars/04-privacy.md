# Pillar 04 — Privacy

## Why it matters

AI systems depend on data. Privacy safeguards reduce legal risk, prevent misuse, and protect individual rights.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Is privacy by design applied in data collection and model development?
- Are lawful bases, purpose limits, and retention periods documented?
- Are PETs considered (anonymization, federated learning, differential privacy)?
- Are cross-border transfer and data sharing controls in place?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How privacy is addressed |
|---|---|
| GDPR-aligned guidance | Sets requirements for lawful processing and data subject rights. |
| EU AI Act ecosystem | Interacts with existing data protection obligations. |
| ISO controls | Supports data governance, access control, and documentation. |
| NIST AI RMF | Encourages privacy risk identification and mitigation. |
