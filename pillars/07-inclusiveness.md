# Pillar 07 — Inclusiveness

## Why it matters

Inclusive AI improves accessibility, usability, and equitable benefit-sharing across diverse populations.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Were impacted groups consulted in system design?
- Are accessibility and language needs considered?
- Are exclusion risks identified for vulnerable users?
- Is feedback from affected communities incorporated?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How inclusiveness is addressed |
|---|---|
| UNESCO/OECD principles | Promote human-centered and inclusive AI development. |
| Public sector guidance | Often requires accessibility and rights-based design. |
| Academic declarations | Emphasize dignity, justice, and participation. |
| Industry governance programs | Include responsible release criteria for user impact. |

## Recommended tools or checklists

- Stakeholder inclusion checklist
- Accessibility review matrix
