# Pillar 05 — Safety and reliability

## Why it matters

Reliable and safe AI reduces harmful failures, improves operational resilience, and supports high-risk deployments.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Are performance thresholds and uncertainty limits defined?
- Are fallback modes and human override options tested?
- Is post-deployment monitoring configured for drift and degradation?
- Are high-risk scenarios stress-tested before release?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How safety/reliability is addressed |
|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF | Focuses on validity, reliability, and risk treatment. |
| ISO/IEC 23894 | Provides AI risk management guidance. |
| Sector regulations | Impose reliability controls in domains like healthcare and finance. |
| Internal assurance frameworks | Define test coverage and release gates. |

## Recommended tools or checklists

- Pre-deployment risk review checklist
- Reliability test matrix
- Continuous monitoring runbook
