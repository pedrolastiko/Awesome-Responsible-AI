# Pillar 08 — Sustainability

## Why it matters

Responsible AI includes environmental and social sustainability, including energy use, emissions, and long-term societal effects.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Are compute and energy footprints measured?
- Is model efficiency considered alongside performance?
- Are lifecycle impacts (training, deployment, retirement) evaluated?
- Are sustainability targets tied to governance decisions?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How sustainability is addressed |
