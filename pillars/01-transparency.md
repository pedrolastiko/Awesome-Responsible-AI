# Pillar 01 — Transparency

## Why it matters

Transparency enables stakeholders to understand when AI is used, how decisions are produced, and which limits apply.

## Key indicators / questions to assess it

- Are users clearly informed that AI contributes to decisions?
- Is model purpose, training scope, and intended use documented?
- Are explainability methods proportionate to risk level?
- Are logs and traceability mechanisms in place for audits?

## Mapping table

| Framework | How transparency is addressed |
|---|---|
| OECD AI principles | Calls for transparency and explainability proportional to context. |
| EU AI Act (implementation context) | Requires transparency duties for specific AI systems and providers. |
| NIST AI RMF | Promotes documentation, communication, and trustworthy characteristics. |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Embeds documentation and control processes in an AI management system. |

## Recommended tools or checklists

- Model cards and system cards
- User-facing AI disclosure checklist
- Traceability/logging checklist
