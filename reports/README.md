# Reports

This directory stores PDF reports referenced in the Awesome Responsible AI list.

## Structure

- `standards-and-frameworks/`: reports related to standards, taxonomies, and institutional frameworks.
- `regulatory-and-legal-compliance/`: reports related to regulatory obligations, AI Act implementation, and supervisory guidance.
- `communities-initiatives-and-continuous-monitoring/`: reports related to pledges, initiatives, and ecosystem monitoring.
- `audit-reporting-and-documentation/`: reports related to transparency reports, assurance artifacts, and governance documentation.
